How it works

  1. Paste keywords

    From Search Console, Google Keyword Planner, autocomplete or any tool export.

  2. We normalise them

    Stop words are removed and plurals/word forms are matched, so “speed up” and “speeding up” group together.

  3. We group by overlap

    Keywords sharing enough meaningful words join one cluster. The strictness slider controls how much overlap is required.

  4. Plan one page each

    The highest-volume keyword becomes the suggested page target; the rest are secondary keywords and headings.

Frequently asked questions

How is search intent decided?

By the words used: “how/what/why/guide” is informational, “best/vs/review” is commercial, “buy/price/order” is transactional, “near me” or a city name is local, and “login/app/official site” is navigational. It is a fast first pass — always look at the actual Google results for important keywords.

Does it group keywords by meaning, like SERP clustering?

It groups by shared meaningful words, not by checking Google results. That makes it free and instant. For the most valuable clusters, confirm by searching the top keywords and checking whether the same pages rank.

What should I set the strictness to?

Around 40–50% works for most lists. Lower it to get fewer, broader topics; raise it to split topics into tighter page-level groups.

Is my keyword list uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser tab.
